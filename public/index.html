How to read this

  • Paid means the payment status is "captured". Pending, attempted, created and failed orders are unpaid.
  • Payment success = paid orders ÷ order attempts. Student conversion = students with at least one paid order ÷ students with at least one order.
  • Students who didn't pay counts students with no paid order inside the selected month, subject and tier.
  • The subject and tier filters keep orders that contain a matching plan. A mixed cart can appear under more than one subject.
  • Revenue, discounts and refunds are not shown: the export has no amount, coupon or refund columns.
  • Channel can't be filtered: the export has no channel, campaign or UTM columns.
  • Test and internal accounts were removed, and plan labels were made consistent. Names, emails and phone numbers are not in this dashboard.
  • On 23 Oct the payment system started using new unpaid statuses (attempted, created, failed) in place of "pending".
